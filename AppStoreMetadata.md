PERMISSIONS
- Camera: only for Scan to PDF, with Apple's document camera (VisionKit). Images stay on the device.

- Office to PDF uses the system's built-in document renderers on the device. PDF to Text saves a plain text file; the app does not create Word documents.

## Review Prompt Strategy (Implemented)

**Trigger conditions:**
- After 2+ successful "good" operations
- Good = compression with >15% reduction, OR any merge, OR any split
- Subsequent prompts only after 4+ months

**When prompted:**
- When user taps "Done" on success screen
- 1.5 second delay to let UI settle

**Never prompt:**
- During an operation
- After a failed operation
- More than ~3 times per year (Apple rate-limits)

**What's New in 2.1**
```
Mino is now a PDF toolkit. Four new tools, all offline:

- Sample flow for Pro: Home > Protect & Unlock > choose a PDF > set a password > Protect PDF shows Mino Pro.
```

**Privacy:** unchanged, "Data Not Collected". New purpose string: `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`.

**What's New in 2.2**

Mino can now scan.

• Scan to PDF: point the camera at a page. Mino finds the edges, straightens the page and captures it. Add as many pages as you need, reorder or retake them, and choose Color, Grayscale or Black & White.
• Recognize Text (Mino Pro): make a scanned PDF searchable, so you can find, select and copy its text. Or save the text on its own.

**Notes for App Review (2.2), additions**

```

**What's New in 2.3**
```
Sign and edit PDFs, offline.

• Fill & Sign (Mino Pro): fill in form fields, then add your signature, initials, the date, text and checkmarks anywhere on the page. Draw your signature once; Mino keeps it on your device only. When you save, everything is fixed in place.
• Edit & Annotate (Mino Pro): highlight, draw, add text and images, cover things with white-out, and add shapes and arrows. Other PDF apps can read your annotations, or you can flatten them into the page.

- Sample flow: Home > Fill & Sign > choose a PDF > Signature > draw > tap the page > Save Signed PDF shows Mino Pro.

**What's New in 2.4**
```
Convert documents, offline.

• Office to PDF (Mino Pro): turn Word, Excel and PowerPoint files into PDFs. Also RTF, text, CSV and HTML. Every sheet of a workbook is included, and each slide gets its own page.
• PDF to Text (Mino Pro): get the words of a PDF in reading order, as a text file you can open in Word, Pages or Notes. Scanned pages are read with text recognition. Best for text documents: layout, tables and pictures are not kept.

Both tools open for everyone, so you can set everything up before deciding.

As always, your files are converted on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Mino Pro is still one purchase, with no subscription.
```

**Notes for App Review (2.4), additions**
```
- Office to PDF uses the system's built-in document renderers on the device. The app makes no network connections.
- PDF to Text saves a plain text file. The app does not create Word documents.
- Sample flow: Home > Office to PDF > choose a .docx file > Convert to PDF shows Mino Pro.
```

**Privacy:** unchanged, "Data Not Collected". No new permission.

**Screenshots:** lead with Office to PDF (`-demoScreen office`) and PDF to Text (`-demoScreen text`).

**Wording to keep:** never "PDF to Word", "to Word", "editable Word document" or "docx export". Say "PDF to Text" and "best for text documents". For Office to PDF, name the formats that were tested (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, RTF, text, CSV, HTML); not Pages, Numbers or Keynote.
